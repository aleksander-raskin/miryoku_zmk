**Thumb keys:**
- 30 = ESC (hold: Media)
- 31 = SPACE (hold: Nav)
- 32 = TAB (hold: Mouse)
- 33 = RET (hold: Sym)
- 34 = BSPC (hold: Num)
- 35 = DEL (hold: Fun)

**Home row mods (your custom order):**
